01

About

I study Biomedical Software Engineering at The Catholic University of Korea, with a double major in Data Science, and I have worked in Prof. Hyo Jung Kim's group since 2025.

Most of my work so far has been infrastructure: building a pipeline that standardizes Korean national drug pricing data, mapping ingredients onto an international terminology standard, and preparing multi-institutional breast cancer records — named-entity recognition, preprocessing, de-identification — so that AI models can be trained on them.

I am interested in how far claims and EMR data can be pushed toward reliable evidence, and in the reproducibility problems that appear when the same clinical concept is written five different ways across five hospitals.
