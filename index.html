Undergraduate Research Assistant, PHI Lab
Biomedical Software Engineering & Data Science — The Catholic University of Korea

Currently working on standardizing national drug pricing data and preparing multi-institutional clinical records for research use, in Python, R, and SQL. Interested in narrowing the gap between what clinical data records and what it can actually tell us.

Research interests

  • Administrative claims data & pharmacoepidemiology

    Using Korean national health insurance data to study how drugs are prescribed, reimbursed, and used in practice.

  • Data normalization & clinical databases

    Mapping local codes and free text onto international terminology standards so that datasets from different institutions can be compared.

  • Clinical Data Science

    Applying statistical and machine learning methods to clinical and administrative records, and checking what those records can reasonably support.

Selected work

2025 – present

  • 2025 – present Drug Reimbursement Code Database for Korean NHIS Drug Information Built a pipeline to standardize drug pricing data and validate ingredients against an international terminology standard.
  • 2025 – present Breast Cancer Clinical Subtype Classification Named-entity recognition and preprocessing on multi-institutional real-world data used for AI-based models.
  • 2025 Digital Media Exposure and Development in Infants and Young Children A national cohort study funded by the Academic Research Scholarship.
